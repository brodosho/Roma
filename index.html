82 BC · the 672nd year since the founding of Rome

EX AGRO

out of the field

You are a tenant farmer nine miles south of Rome. You own one tunic, one clay oven and one field of hard wheat, and you owe rent on the field.

Above you sit landowners, merchants, magistrates, consuls and generals. Above all of them, one man.

Rome, this yearSulla has marched on Rome, made himself dictator and posted lists of men who may be killed for their property. The city holds close to a million people. Almost none of them own anything.
